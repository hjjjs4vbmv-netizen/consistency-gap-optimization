\subsection{Exact local structure under one-sided stop-gradient training}
\label{sec:exact-local-structure}

The detached target must be represented in the training derivative.  Let
$\widetilde\ell(\theta,\bar\theta)$ denote a lifted loss whose target branch
uses $\bar\theta$.  Its one-sided stop-gradient derivative is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{sg}}_\theta\ell
  :=\left.\partial_\theta
  \widetilde\ell(\theta,\bar\theta)
  \right|_{\bar\theta=\theta},
  \label{eq:one-sided-sg-derivative}
\end{equation}
which differs from the ordinary total derivative along the diagonal
$\bar\theta=\theta$.

For the Euclidean residual in Equation~\eqref{eq:spacing-objective}, define
$e_r=D_t-T_r$, $u_r=e_r/\lVert e_r\rVert_2$, and
$J_t=\partial_\theta D_t$.  Away from $e_r=0$, the implemented training field
at a common state is exactly
\begin{equation}
  g(r,\Delta)
  :=\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{sg}}_\theta\ell(r,\Delta)
  =\frac{1}{\Delta}J_t^\top u_r.
  \label{eq:one-sided-ect-field}
\end{equation}
Compare a reference pair $(r_1,\Delta_1)$ with an intervened pair
$(r_g,\Delta_g)$ at the same online parameters, data, noise, and stochastic
realization.  With $s=\Delta_1/\Delta_g$, direct substitution gives
\begin{equation}
  g_g
  =s g_1
   +\frac{1}{\Delta_g}J_t^\top
     \bigl(u_{r_g}-u_{r_1}\bigr).
  \label{eq:exact-local-composite}
\end{equation}
The first term is explicit weight rescaling.  The second is the complete
target-endpoint correction at that state.  The identity is finite-spacing and
uses no small-gap or optimizer approximation.

The target-by-denominator factorial makes the two channels operational.  Let
A use the reference target and denominator, B use the intervened target and
denominator, C use the intervened target with the reference denominator, and
D use the reference target with the intervened denominator.  At a matched
state and sample,
\begin{equation}
  g_B=s g_C,
  \qquad
  g_D=s g_A,
  \qquad
  g_B-g_D=s(g_C-g_A).
  \label{eq:exact-factorial-identities}
\end{equation}
These identities factorize the intervention at the objective-gradient level.
They do not imply an additive, seed-invariant, or endpoint-quality
factorization after the arms follow separate training trajectories.
