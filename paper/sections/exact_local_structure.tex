\subsection{Exact local structure under one-sided stop-gradient training}
\label{sec:exact-local-structure}

The detached target must be represented in the training derivative.  Let
$\widetilde\ell(\theta,\bar\theta)$ denote a lifted loss whose target branch
uses $\bar\theta$.  Its one-sided stop-gradient derivative is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{sg}}_\theta\ell
  :=\left.\partial_\theta
  \widetilde\ell(\theta,\bar\theta)
  \right|_{\bar\theta=\theta},
  \label{eq:one-sided-sg-derivative}
\end{equation}
which differs from the ordinary total derivative along the diagonal
$\bar\theta=\theta$.

For the objective in Equation~\eqref{eq:spacing-objective}, define
$\psi_r=\nabla_e\rho_c(e)|_{e=e_r}$ and $J_t=\partial_\theta D_t$.  For the
smoothed norm used here,
\begin{equation}
  \psi_r
  =\frac{e_r}{\sqrt{\lVert e_r\rVert_2^2+c^2}}.
  \label{eq:smoothed-residual-score}
\end{equation}
When $c=0$ and $e_r\neq0$, this reduces to the unit vector
$u_r=e_r/\lVert e_r\rVert_2$.  Provided that $w(t,\Delta)$ is independent of
$\theta$, the implemented training field at a common state is exactly
\begin{equation}
  g(r,\Delta)
  :=\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{sg}}_\theta\ell(r,\Delta)
  =w(t,\Delta)J_t^\top\psi_r.
  \label{eq:one-sided-ect-field}
\end{equation}
Compare a reference pair $(r_1,\Delta_1)$ with an intervened pair
$(r_g,\Delta_g)$ at the same online parameters, data, noise, and stochastic
realization.  Let $w_1=w(t,\Delta_1)$, $w_g=w(t,\Delta_g)$, and
$s=w_g/w_1$.  Direct substitution gives
\begin{equation}
  g_g
  =s g_1
   +w_gJ_t^\top
     \bigl(\psi_{r_g}-\psi_{r_1}\bigr).
  \label{eq:exact-local-composite}
\end{equation}
The first term records any explicit weight rescaling.  The second is the
complete target-endpoint correction at that state.  The identity is
finite-spacing and uses no small-gap or optimizer approximation.

The target-by-weight factorial makes the two channels operational.  Let A use
the reference target and weight, B use the intervened target and weight, C use
the intervened target with the reference weight, and D use the reference
target with the intervened weight.  At a matched state and sample,
\begin{equation}
  g_B=s g_C,
  \qquad
  g_D=s g_A,
  \qquad
  g_B-g_D=s(g_C-g_A).
  \label{eq:exact-factorial-identities}
\end{equation}
These identities factorize the intervention at the objective-gradient level.
They do not imply an additive, seed-invariant, or endpoint-quality
factorization after the arms follow separate training trajectories.

The weighting regime determines the scalar factor.  In legacy inverse-gap
ECT, $w=1/\Delta$ and $s=\Delta_1/\Delta_g$.  In the PR~\#91 ImageNet setting,
$w=\omega_{\mathrm{snr}+k}(t)$ at matched $t$, so $w_g=w_1$ and $s=1$.
Consequently, the longitudinal IA--IB comparison in PR~\#91 is a
target-endpoint intervention at the local objective-field level; it is not a
target-by-denominator factorial.
