## M1: the pooled H mixes two distinct seed-level structures

### The problem

The primary quantity is the per-seed endpoint history contrast

    H = 0.5 * [(Y_BA - Y_AA) + (Y_BB - Y_AB)]

with Y the 1024-kimg log-FID at NFE1. H < 0 reads as "B history better at
the endpoint". But H < 0 arises from two different histories, distinguished
by the switch-point gap Q = logFID_B@512 - logFID_A@512:

| Path | Switch point (512) | Endpoint (1024) | Nature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advantage persistence | B already ahead (Q < 0) | B still ahead (H < 0) | Mid-training ranking was **correct**; no reversal |
| Delayed reversal | B worse (Q > 0) | B ahead (H < 0) | Mid-training ranking was **wrong** — the misranking phenomenon |

The pooled mean adds the two together. Only the second bears on the
mid-training-misranking reading; the first is precisely the case where the
mid-training FID ranking did **not** mislead.

### What the diagnostic shows

**(a) H is positively associated with Q**: Pearson corr(H, Q) = +0.598
(Spearman +0.345, OLS slope +0.120). A positive sign means the seeds with
the largest endpoint B-history advantage are the seeds where B already led
most at the switch — the opposite of what a reversal-driven pooled effect
would produce.

**(b) Two seeds dominate the pooled mean**:

| Subgroup | Seeds | n | H mean | 95% CI | H < 0 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Q < -0.5 (B far ahead at switch) | 31, 40 | 2 | -0.296 | [-0.764, +0.173] | 2/2 |
| All others | 32-37, 39, 41, 42 | 9 | -0.027 | [-0.065, **+0.012**] | 6/9 |
| Q < 0 (B ahead, all) | 31, 36, 39, 40, 42 | 5 | -0.134 | [-0.326, +0.058] | 4/5 |
| Q > 0 (B worse, all) | 32, 33, 34, 35, 37, 41 | 6 | -0.027 | [-0.084, +0.029] | 4/6 |

Excluding seed31 (Q = -0.634, H = -0.332) and seed40 (Q = -1.196,
H = -0.259) shrinks the mean from -0.076 to -0.027 with a zero-crossing CI.
The primary leave-one-seed-out means already expose this: dropping seed31 or
seed40 yields the two weakest of the eleven LOSO means (-0.0499, -0.0572).

### How to cite

- "8/11 negative" must not be cited alone: four of the eight negative signs
  come from the Q < 0 group (advantage persistence), only four from
  reversal-eligible seeds.
- Any manuscript use of the pooled H must carry this decomposition, or
  present the subgroups directly.
- The 0.5 threshold is not post hoc: the script reports the symmetric 0 and
  +/-0.5 thresholds in full, both sides under the same rule. 0.5 log-FID is
  a ~65% FID ratio, far above the evaluation noise floor.

## M2: replication status of the delayed rank reversal

### The problem

The direct evidential form of the misranking phenomenon is: B worse at the
switch (Q > 0), then overtaking at the endpoint under the controlled matched
continuation (H < 0). The discovery cohort (seeds 5, 6, 7) gave 3/3
reversals at magnitudes -0.33 to -0.95 log. The fresh cohort's direct test
of this phenomenon sits in the per-seed CSV; `REPORT_11SEED.md` does not
single it out. This note states it explicitly.

### What the diagnostic shows

Reversal-eligible set = seeds with Q > 0; a reversal = H < 0 for that seed.

Q > 0.5 (switch-point gap clearly above noise): **2/3 reversed**

| seed | Q (switch B-A) | H (endpoint) | Call |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 34 | +0.975 | -0.087 | reversed |
| 41 | +0.535 | -0.090 | reversed |
| 35 | +0.568 | **+0.035** | **not reversed** |

Q > 0 (all eligible): **4/6 reversed** (above plus seed33 Q = +0.089 ->
H = -0.022 reversed; seed37 Q = +0.047 -> H = -0.027 reversed; seed32
Q = +0.008 -> H = +0.028 not reversed; the latter three have Q near the
noise floor, so their eligibility is itself weak).

### How to cite

- **Direction survives weakly; magnitude does not**: reversals recur (2/3 in
  the strong-eligibility set) but at roughly -0.09 — about one quarter to
  one tenth of the discovery magnitudes (-0.33 to -0.95).
- **seed35 is the first clear counterexample**: a switch-point gap of +0.57
  (well above noise) with B history still worse at the endpoint (+0.035).
  No such seed exists in the discovery cohort. Any statement that reversal
  is a regularity is therefore unsupported; the supported form is frequency-
  based: discovery 3/3 plus fresh strong-eligibility 2/3 (5/6 combined),
  with fresh magnitudes an order of magnitude smaller.
- Citation rule: every reversal statement must carry the eligibility
  definition (Q threshold), the frequency, **and** the magnitudes. Frequency
  alone is not permitted.

## One-sentence joint reading

About two thirds of the fresh pooled H (-0.076) is contributed by
persistence of an advantage B already held at the switch, which is
irrelevant to the misranking phenomenon; the strong-eligibility subset that
directly tests the phenomenon gives 2/3 reversals at ~-0.09 — direction
alive, magnitude unreplicated, and a first clear counterexample (seed35) on
record.
