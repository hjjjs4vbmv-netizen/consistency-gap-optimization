## Coverage conclusion

The cited record covers fast consistency generation, improved/direct consistency training, target/coupling variance, adaptive traversal/discretization, conditional theory, and adaptive-optimizer state. The manuscript's bounded novelty is the conjunction of an exact ECT pair-spacing decomposition, a target-by-denominator factorial, and budget-resolved evidence showing phase advances, ties, reversals, and changing rankings. No citation is used to imply cross-$q$, cross-dataset, or universal optimizer conclusions.
